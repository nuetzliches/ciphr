    How to read it

    • built, not switchable
    • a property of the deployment, not code
    • switchable (surface entry)
    • not built, or deferred
    • reviewed core — a band, not a ring
    • a cut: ignores every ring

    Rings are not quality levels. Each ring is a boundary with a gate, and its label says what is checked. Outwards it is not the quality that decreases but the number of parties that increases.
